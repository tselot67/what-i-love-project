Meet the Person Behind the Stitches

Hello! I'm Tselot, a crochet lover and handmade creations enthusiast. I started crocheting when I was in high school, and it quickly became something I truly enjoy. Crocheting gives me a feeling of relaxation and allows me to take a break from my busy days. I love turning simple yarn into something beautiful and meaningful with my own hands.
